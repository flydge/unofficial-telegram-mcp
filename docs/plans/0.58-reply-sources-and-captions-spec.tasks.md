## Part 1 — freeze and ladder table (work items 1 and 2)

**Freeze.** `tests/reply_ladder_matrix.py` generated `tests/fixtures/reply_ladder_matrix_0_57.json` once at the
0.57 tip (f2239c4), before any production change. `tests/test_reply_ladder_matrix.py` pins it by sha256 and
compares freshly computed rows byte for byte. The 6,098 rows cover:
- every subset of the eight span families × {text, document, photo, voice note} × the datetime levels (absent,
  plain, relative, absolute, all three);
- sanitizer-rewritten bodies and forum-topic sources;
- CustomEmoji and MediaTimestamp probes, all refused at 0.57;
- the inline v23 TextUrl literals;
- every row of the eight existing reply goldens.

Each row records the version, the capability tuple, `is_media`, `sanitized`, sha256 digests cut to 16 hex
digits of the projection, the display and one fixed text-reply preview, and every forged version in 0..31 that
the stored projection would also accept (none).

**Ladder table.** `src/telegram_search_mcp/reply_source_ladder.py` is the one owner of each family's name,
keyword, capability, entity map, rank, text and media version, overlap exclusivity and inert display, and of
the fixed capability listing order. Version selection, presence, capability tuples, stored-version admission and
the shims' keyword admission all derive from it. The keyword signatures of `formatted_text`, `validate_content`,
`render_formatted_text`, `render_content` and the media `media_content`, `validate_content` and `render_content`
stay as shims over new `*_for(…, admission)` functions. `_boundaries` and `_inert_json_string` stay in
`reply_formatted_sources`.

**Gate.** The full isolated-HOME suite at 85ef050 gave 4118 passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped, 20830 subtests passed in
2216.07s. That is the 0.57 baseline of 4106 plus the 2 matrix and 10 ladder tests.

Lead decisions, owner review pending:
- **Refusals are not frozen by message.** A refused input records only nulls, because refusal messages are
  internal and the broker maps them all to `unavailable`. The media "disagrees" messages merged into one.
- **The stored-version check replaces the per-family caption checks.** A stored media projection validates under
  its top family and every family below it, and its validated caption must select the stored version. This
  replaces the seven per-family checks and the v12/v14 special cases. The keyword shims keep their 0.57 rule:
  each flagged family must be present.
- **The datetime levels are table rows.** Relative and Absolute are rows that refine DateTime by formatting type.
  They share `reply_datetime_targets` and are absent from the listing order.
- **Exclusivity and inert display are table fields.** They are per-family policy, so a new family sets them in
  its row.
- **The media display fails closed on an inert family.** It refuses until it gets the whole-record inert path,
  so giving TextUrl a media version cannot silently sanitize hidden URLs. This is unreachable at 0.57.
- **The preview digest is the text-reply digest only.** The reply-artifact facts move to v2 in part 3, which
  would otherwise change a frozen value that has nothing to do with the ladder.

Seams for parts 2 and 3:
- A new family is a `Family` row: a rank above TextUrl, `text_version` or `media_version` set to `None` where
  the family is refused, placed at the front of `LISTING_ORDER`, and `exclusive=True`. v27 stays refused because
  no row claims it.
- Media TextUrl (v24) means setting `TEXT_URL.media_version` and replacing the fail-closed branch of
  `reply_media_sources._render` with the inert record.
- Flipped 0.57 probes go into `FLIPS` in `tests/test_reply_ladder_matrix.py`, each with its reason. A flip may only
  admit an input that 0.57 refused, and every other row must stay byte-identical. `SPANS` in
  `tests/test_reply_source_ladder.py` needs a builder for each new family.
- Part 3 (formatted captions) does not depend on the ladder. Its reply-artifact preview change sits outside the
  frozen matrix.
